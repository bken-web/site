# BKEN Brand Guidelines

## Visual Identity
BKEN's visual identity is built on a foundation of **Technical Precision** and **Cinematic Authority**.

### 🎨 Color Palette
- **Main Blue (Accent):** `#3A7BD5`
- **Core Black:** `#000000`
- **Slate (Surface):** `#0F172A`
- **Text Primary:** `#FFFFFF`
- **Text Secondary:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`

### Typography
- **Display/Headings:** Poppins (Bold, Tracking -4%)
- **Serif Accents:** Lora (Italic, Medium)
- **Technical/Mono:** JetBrains Mono (Tracking 20%)
- **Body:** Poppins (Regular/Medium)

### Logo Usage
- **b.ken:** Used for header and main branding.
- **b.:** Used for shortcuts, social avatars, and high-impact hero moments.
- **Clearance:** Maintain a minimum clearance equal to the width of the 'b' character.

## Voice & Tone
- **Institutional Authority:** We speak as experts in the financial technology sector.
- **Precise & Secure:** High-conviction statements backed by technical literacy.
- **Cinematic & Educational:** Evocative storytelling that makes finance accessible.
- **Modern:** Forward-thinking solutions for traditional institutions.
